## Why this exists

Some of this code is open (Apache-2.0), some is private for now and may later be opened under
AGPL-3.0 with a paid commercial licence beside it (ecosystem decision 87). To keep both possible, we
need your permission to use your contribution under any of those licences. This agreement gives
that permission. **You keep your copyright; you give us a licence.**

"We" means Kashif Shahzad, and — from the day it is registered — Indorania (SMC-Private) Limited,
which takes over this agreement without any further step (decision 68). Anyone who later takes over
the project from us does too.

## 1. What is covered

A "contribution" is any code, text, design, data or other work that you intentionally send to us
for one of these repositories — a pull request, a patch, an issue with code in it, an email. It
covers what you have sent before you sign, and everything you send afterwards, until you tell us in
writing that you stop (that stops only future contributions).

## 2. You keep your copyright

Nothing here transfers your copyright to us. You can still use your own work however you like.

We will keep your name with your contribution in the history and changelog unless you ask us not

## 6. What you promise

- The contribution is your own work, or you have the right to give it to us on these terms.
- If your employer, university or client could claim rights in it, they have agreed in writing that
